Key Analytical Finding & Business Impact

Analyzing consecutive stage transitions exposes two distinct revenue leaks: 742 users abandon their carts before initiating checkout (45.3% drop-off), and 421 users drop off during payment processing (47.0% drop-off). Addressing mobile payment friction directly targets this second major leakage point.
